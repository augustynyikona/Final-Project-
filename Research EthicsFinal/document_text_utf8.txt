Delimitations [2 â€“ 3 paragraphs]The scheduling of pediatric vaccinations at Institute 1 and Institute 2 in Ndola, Zambia, will be the exclusive subject of this study. In order to maintain a narrow scope that enables in-depth investigation of pediatric immunization operations, the researcher purposefully leaves out private clinics and adult vaccination services.
